CustomChat is a configurable chat app over your sources. Ask questions, follow the citations, and choose the model and look that fit your work.

WHAT CUSTOMCHAT DOES

A closer look.

Illustrations of the tools, not live answers.

01 / SOURCES
Source → citation

Grounded in sources

Open a citation to inspect the evidence.

02 / CONVERSATION
Question → follow-up

Built for follow-ups

Saved chats carry recent context. Earlier answers are not source evidence.

03 / YOUR APP
Your setup, your style

Made to be yours

Configure sources, models, wording and themes.

04 / READING
A comfortable reading view

Read your way

Change text size and answer width. Switch light and dark themes.

05 / ACTIONS
Preview → confirm → done

Review, then act

Preview a local note action. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

06 / TAKE IT WITH YOU
From conversation to something useful

Keep a useful answer

Copy an answer or export it as Markdown, PDF or references.
